# A mapping from WISEDash Local labels to StriveTogether ones.

A mapping from WISEDash Local labels to StriveTogether ones.

## Usage

``` r
GLOSSARY_OF_POPS_FOR_WISEDASH_LOCAL
```

## Format

### GLOSSARY_OF_POPS_FOR_WISEDASH_LOCAL

An object of class `spec_tbl_df` (inherits from `tbl_df`, `tbl`,
`data.frame`) with 14 rows and 4 columns.

- GROUP_BY:

  `<chr>` broader demographic concepts such as Race/Ethnicity, Gender,
  or SES.

- GROUP_BY_VALUE:

  `<chr>` specific instances of groups such as Race/Ethnicity, Gender,
  or SES.

- Gender:

  `<chr>` currently, Strive just uses two, plus "All"

- Population:

  `<chr>` a specific socioeconomic status, race/ethnicity, or gender
